This list is based on my Skoob backup and contains 67 books. I read most of them in Portuguese, so the first list gives English readers the works' official English titles, while the second records the Portuguese editions in my reading history. When I could not confirm an official English-language edition, I kept the Portuguese title rather than inventing one.

## Books That Became Posts

- **The Burnout Society — Byung-Chul Han**: the main book behind [Why Are We Always Tired: Han's Diagnosis](/en/posts/why-are-we-always-tired-hans-diagnosis/).
- **Antifragile — Nassim Nicholas Taleb**: became my notes in [How to Benefit from Chaos: Notes on Taleb's 'Antifragile'](/en/posts/how-to-benefit-from-chaos-notes-on-talebs-antifragile/).
- **Twilight of the Idols — Friedrich Nietzsche** and **Notes from Underground — Fyodor Dostoevsky**: appear in [A Parallel Between Nietzsche and Dostoevsky](/en/posts/a-parallel-between-nietzsche-and-dostoevsky/).
- **Why We Sleep — Matthew Walker**: a complementary book cited in [Why Are We Always Tired: Han's Diagnosis](/en/posts/why-are-we-always-tired-hans-diagnosis/).
