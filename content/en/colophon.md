You found a note tucked between the books.

FariaBlog is a house for reading. Home, Writing, Books and About are views of the same place. When an essay begins, the interior gives way to paper.

The pages are built with Hugo. Georgia gives the writing its voice; pale paper and oxblood accompany the reading. The house images have three lighting variants, chosen using each visitor's local hour: noon, dusk and midnight.

The small window in the mark has one partly open leaf. An invitation to look outside — and return within.
